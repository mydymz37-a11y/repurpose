Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains how RepurposeKit handles information when you use the service.

Information and account data

When you sign in, the authentication service processes the account information needed to create and maintain your session. If you save a project, its source text, settings, generated output and related project details are stored so you can access that project from your account. Do not save information you do not have permission to use.

AI provider API keys

You may save your own OpenAI or Google Gemini API key in account settings. The key is encrypted server-side using AES-GCM before storage, is scoped to your signed-in account, and is never returned to the browser after saving. Generation requests are sent server-side to the selected AI provider using your saved key. You pay the provider directly for usage under its terms and billing. You can replace or delete the key in your account settings. The app requires a server encryption secret configured by the project owner; keys cannot be saved if that secret is unavailable.

Source extraction and uploaded files

Article URL extraction and YouTube caption retrieval send the URL you submit to RepurposeKit's server-side extraction endpoints so they can attempt to retrieve publicly accessible text. Uploaded TXT, Markdown, PDF and DOCX files are processed in your browser in the current application; extracted text may be included in a project if you choose to save it. Scanned PDFs may not yield readable text.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, save and retrieve your projects, process extraction requests, and maintain the service. This page does not make claims about specific retention periods, deletion timelines, analytics, or third-party infrastructure beyond the behavior described above.

Third-party services

The service relies on Hatchable hosting/authentication, and may connect your browser to OpenAI or Google when you choose an AI provider. Their services are governed by their own policies. External libraries may be loaded from public CDNs.

Your choices and requests

You can avoid saving sensitive source material, use your own provider settings, and contact support with privacy questions or requests. We will review requests and respond where reasonably possible; applicable legal rights depend on your location.
