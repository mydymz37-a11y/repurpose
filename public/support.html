Common troubleshooting

  • AI generation fails: confirm your API key, provider, selected model, and provider account access or balance.
  • Article or YouTube extraction fails: the source may block automated access or captions may be unavailable. Paste the text or transcript manually.
  • PDF upload has no text: scanned/image-only PDFs may require OCR before uploading.
  • Project does not save: confirm that you are signed in and try again. Include the error message in your support email.

Support is provided by email. A response time is not guaranteed at this stage.
