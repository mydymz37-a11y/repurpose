Frequently asked questions

Do I pay RepurposeKit for AI?

No. You use your own OpenAI or Gemini API key and pay the provider directly for usage.

Is my API key stored?

Yes. Your key is stored encrypted on the server, linked to your account, and is not sent back to your browser.

Can it automatically extract every article or YouTube transcript?

Not reliably. Paste the article text or transcript when the source blocks automated access.

Are the testimonials real?

No testimonials are presented as real. Any sample quotes in future marketing must be labelled as examples.
